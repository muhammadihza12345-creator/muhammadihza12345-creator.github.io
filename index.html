Turn long videos into short clips, and post them on schedule

Auto Clipper finds the best moments in a long video, cuts them into vertical 9:16 clips with subtitles, and publishes them to the creator's own TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook accounts at the times they choose.

Features

Highlight finder

Transcribes the video and picks 15-60 second moments that stand on their own, with a strong opening and a clear ending.

Vertical reframing

Crops to 9:16 and follows the speaker's face, or fits the full frame over a blurred background for slides and group shots.

Readable subtitles

Word-by-word captions burned into the clip, plus an optional title line and cover image.

Post text

A title, description and hashtags for each platform, ready before anything is published.

Review before posting

Every clip is sent to the owner for approval first. Nothing is published without the owner's OK.

Scheduled publishing

Approved clips are published to the connected accounts at chosen times, with a status message after each post.

How it works

  1. Add a video. Paste a video link or choose a file.
  2. Clips are made. Highlights are found, reframed, subtitled and given a cover and post text.
  3. Review. The owner watches each clip and approves or skips it.
  4. Connect accounts. The owner signs in once with each platform's official login (TikTok Login Kit, Google, Meta) and grants permission to post.
  5. Publish. Approved clips are uploaded to the owner's own accounts at the scheduled times.

TikTok integration

Auto Clipper uses TikTok Login Kit to let the account owner authorize the app, and the Content Posting API to upload the owner's approved clips to their own TikTok account with the caption they reviewed. It reads only basic profile information (display name) to show which account is connected. It never posts to accounts that have not authorized it, and access can be revoked at any time in TikTok's settings.

Your data

Videos, clips and login tokens stay on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

FAQ

Who can use Auto Clipper?

It is run by its owner on their own computer to publish to accounts they own and have connected. There is no public sign-up.

Does it post without asking?

No. Each clip is approved by the owner before it is scheduled.

Which platforms are supported?

TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels, through each platform's official API.

How do I disconnect an account?

Remove the app's access in that platform's settings (in TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services). The app then stops posting to that account.

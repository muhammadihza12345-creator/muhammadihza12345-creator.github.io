Auto Clipper

Auto Clipper is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It turns long videos into short vertical clips and publishes them to the owner's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram on a schedule.

It is not a public service: no one else can sign up, and it only ever posts to accounts its owner has connected and authorized.

What it does

  • Finds highlights in a video and makes 9:16 clips with subtitles and a thumbnail.
  • Uploads each clip with its title, description and hashtags to the owner's connected accounts.
  • Sends the owner a status message after each upload.

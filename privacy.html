Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Auto Clipper ("the app") is a personal tool operated by its owner for their own social media accounts. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos and set thumbnails on the owner's channel, and to read the channel's name to confirm the right channel is connected.
  • TikTok: basic profile information (display name, open ID) and permission to upload videos or send them to the owner's TikTok drafts.
  • Facebook and Instagram (Meta): the list of Pages the owner manages, the linked Instagram professional account, and permission to publish videos to them.

How data is used and stored

  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own accounts.
  • The app has no server and no database. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • Upload results (post IDs and links) are kept in a local file on the owner's computer and sent to the owner's private notification channel.

Google API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Google: remove "Auto Clipper" at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Apps and services permissions, then remove "Auto Clipper".
  • Facebook/Instagram: Settings, then Business integrations (or Apps and websites), then remove "Auto Clipper".
  • Deleting the app's local folder on the owner's computer removes all stored tokens and records. You can also email the contact below to request deletion.
